Reference run

This page summarises reference run RR1: one emergency department (ED) chest pain case, compiled from its case blueprint into a synced multi-station practice circuit. Its published cuts follow the learner from doorway to encounter at Station 1, then doorway to encounter at Station 2, before the faculty sign-off acknowledgement.

Chapter 1 — Doorway and Station 1 of 3

The learner enters and sees Station 1 of 3 from the server.

One server rotates the learner through the stations of a circuit, one station at a time, starting here.

The station comes from the case blueprint: actors, dialogue seeds, rubric labels and trace come from the scenario.

Chapter 2 — Encounter and Station 1 of 3

The learner speaks; speech-to-text (STT) on the host transcribes the question, the actor answers with synthesized speech, and asks to repeat when confidence is low.

Actor speech is synthesized in the browser with text-to-speech (TTS).

Each turn is recorded with modality and confidence; replay captions are recomputed from the trace.

The published encounter cut shows Station 1 of 3 from the server; the faculty observer stays read-only.

Chapter 3 — Doorway and Station 2 of 3

The published doorway cut shows Station 2 of 3 after the blueprint break.

Encounter controls follow the server into the next station.

Chapter 4 — Encounter and Station 2 of 3

The next published cut shows the learner in the Station 2 encounter.

The phase and encounter controls remain server-authoritative after rotation.

Chapter 5 — Faculty sign-off acknowledged by the server (protocol probe; no faculty console UI yet)

Stations replay from their recorded traces over the protocol (protocol today; faculty review UI pending).

Rubric marks are entered against replayable station traces, and a sign-off packet locks them (protocol today; faculty review UI pending).

The published cut ends after the server acknowledges the finalized sign-off.
