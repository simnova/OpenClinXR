## Authority and finish line

This contract governs the new SC task package. It preserves the scope of the [research brief](../humanoid-scene-layout-research-brief-2026-09-09.md) while correcting the completion claims in the [earlier implementation plan](../scene-layout-implementation-plan-2026-09-09.md). Existing repository policy remains authoritative. Each SC card repeats its essential outcome, controls and exclusions. If a card omits a requirement, the requirement remains OPEN; a worker must report the mismatch, not interpret omission as permission to drop it. Do not edit this contract or weaken a frozen card to make results pass.

Finish line: **one persisted, approved synthetic encounter loads its declared room and starting state; actual readiness controls entry; the patient stays supported supine; its selected physician approaches and stops at an accepted bedside target with appropriate equipment access; the same versioned result replays; an uninterrupted normal-workflow recording and measured evidence support a truthful demonstration on the existing website.** Component cards, unit tests, a moving model or a polished clip alone do not close this outcome.

The technical prototype and website integration must be demonstrated. Public deployment is a separate explicit release decision. Clinical usefulness needs qualified review; no claim of clinical validity, exam equivalence or Quest readiness follows from desktop evidence. A pending clinical or headset assessment must stay visible and prevents the corresponding claim, rather than becoming a fictional pass.

## Readiness and lifecycle rules

Initial specifications are pure compiled intent. Observe actual consumers for each predicate, keyed by entity/instance, version and capability; never alternate labels by index or treat a supplied asset-ID set as proof of power, connection, support or loading. Bind every acknowledgment to the active session/run, case and requirement-schema revision, actual instance/asset revision and observation time. Reject client-invented, stale, wrong-run or missing observations at the API owner. Keep satisfied/unsatisfied/pending/unknown distinct from asset-load statuses. Represent the expected INITIAL predicate separately from the learner GOAL: observed `connected=false` can satisfy the initial requirement while the later goal `connected=true` remains incomplete. Unknown/pending is not proof of verified incompleteness. Observe both normal mount and suppression/fallback branches. A normal load signal with a fallback still visible does not satisfy exact authored intent.

Enforce this in the existing preparation/admission owners and transport, not by putting lifecycle calls into the pure specification module. **No new phase does not mean no enforcement.** Prevent API bypass as well as UI entry bypass. Preserve current domain time; take the accepted snapshot before scheduled effects begin. Define effect acknowledgment, deduplication and retry/failure behavior so marking an event emitted cannot discard a failed effect. After acceptance, asset removal/replacement or changed requirements invalidate the acknowledgment and accepted plan; stop/refuse approach until the new state is accepted through the normal owner. Keep raw observation state under existing runtime ownership and durable review/replay records; do not build a parallel state engine.

Supported preview may remain available while rejected, but must explicitly say it is not an accepted learner encounter. A generated static bundle with `learner_use_blocked`, `metadata_only` evidence or a review-only route is not positive A01–A12 proof.

## Geometry and motion rules

Use an exact support instance, world-metre orthonormal frame and actual normalized mounted geometry. Keep parent/child transforms explicit and do not double-apply GLB scale. Persist intent through case, compile, bundle, slot repair, load and animation; do not re-read fixture defaults over a persisted case. Standing requires a named floor frame. Observe actual anatomy and equipment configuration rather than substituting fixed ED coordinates or one adult proxy for every actor.

A sampled route may be an implementation stage, but acceptance must detect thin obstacles between prior 0.35 m samples and test swept occupied volume including actor dimensions. Validate after asset replacement and layout changes. Runtime must verify the plan against its bound observed geometry revision or recompute; a supplied clear flag, empty obstacle list or forged plan is insufficient. A change during travel must stop/invalidate the approach. Do not claim a route clear from empty obstacle input.

Engineering starting limits for the bounded demo: horizontal arrival error at most 0.05 m; settled body-heading error at most 10 degrees; no resumed root travel during a two-second stopped observation. Clinical reach/access distances must be authored and reviewed for the chosen task, not borrowed from a universal percentile table. SC-00, after selected assets are pinned and before SC-03/05 are released, independently freezes support/contact, penetration, foot-slide, deformation and timing thresholds in a versioned rubric using the unchanged control and deliberately broken examples. It must reject visible defects, absent contact windows and zero sampled bodies. Measure signed contact height relative to the selected floor frame with a separate penetration metric; deeply submerged feet cannot count as successful stance. The first slice may support only its selected flat frame and must reject unsupported floor geometry. Include a penetrating-foot and between-waypoint obstacle counterexample. The implementer cannot enlarge thresholds after seeing a failure; any change requires a documented independent review and re-running all controls. These are engineering checks, not clinical standards.

## Evidence and video contract

Run the normal main UI-XR workflow from the persisted case and generated bundle. Camera positioning, ordinary clicks and repeatable inputs are allowed. Capture code may observe exposed telemetry; it must not write transforms, call the approach executor directly, mark readiness, inject a plan/locomotion global or skip production consumers.

The uninterrupted source video begins before encounter activation and includes room realization, entry, support, approach, arrival and the stopped observation. The accompanying report identifies scenario/version, case and bundle hashes, code revision, asset/clip/rig/solver hashes, instance/actor IDs, seed/index, request/run/replay IDs, timestamps and actual phase/domain time. Measurements and frames must refer to the same run; attach camera/viewport/capture settings and missing/dropped-frame information. Retain negative recordings for required unsupported state, wrong/missing support and blocked approach, plus a successful recovery/replay through supported paths.

Store raw working traces/videos in ignored `.agent-factory/browser-evidence/scene-closure/` or an equivalent existing ignored evidence root. SC-07 must first pin an authorized existing storage location, access policy, retention owner and period and demonstrate independent retrieval. The existing local volume outside disposable worktrees is the default; new cloud/paid hosting/public upload is not authorized. Before SC-07 can close, retain originals and reports at a durable restricted artifact location accessible to the reviewer, with hashes and retention recorded; ignored local work directories alone are insufficient. Only cleared, sanitized, compact artifacts enter `docs/assets/` or the established public video directory. Record actual retrievable artifact locations, sizes and content hashes; promised URLs and machine-local paths do not satisfy final delivery. Keep raw hidden case findings, tokens, credentials and personal data out of public material. Test sanitization with synthetic sentinel content; do not place real hidden facts in tests or reports. A reviewer watches source and edited recordings; JSON alone cannot close visual quality.

The website edit derives solely from the accepted source run, records edit ranges and includes captions/transcript, poster and accessible controls. State desktop-browser evidence clearly; do not claim autonomous behavior from an injected drive. Public summary may omit sensitive measurement detail while retaining a verified link to appropriate sanitized full proof. Performance budgets and video size/encoding are fixed in SC-08 before committing media.

Preparation is local/review-branch work; do not push/merge/publicly deploy as an incidental evidence or documentation step. ## Execution and closing discipline

Each task must add or extend behavior tests through the production entry/caller it owns. Record a genuine before/after or remove-the-fix counterweight; a source-string assertion, empty list, direct pure-helper invocation or expected-failure marker left in the completed acceptance path cannot establish integration. Build changed package exports before cross-package checks; check the actual consumer entry, not stale `dist/`. No new root barrel may pull `node:crypto` or other server-only code into the browser.

Independent SC-09 review examines every matrix row and replays proof. Required failures keep that row open and return to its owner. Component scope may land while parent acceptance remains open. Human clinical review and worn-headset evidence are required before their corresponding claims; absence does not authorize invented sign-off. The SC-10 research lane cannot block honest baseline demonstration merely because an optional model is unavailable, nor close a quality comparison without execution.
