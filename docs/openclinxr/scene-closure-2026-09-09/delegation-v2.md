## Failure and correction loop

A failed required check keeps its A-row and the parent open. Capture the exact failing observation and return it to the original implementation owner; QA/video/site tasks must not secretly fix product movement or relax thresholds. For an unlanded card, record the failed treatment and continue that same bounded task through the normal mailbox/review loop. If the original card is already integrated, the owner creates a narrowly scoped corrective card referencing the failed A-row, original card and unchanged acceptance requirements; keep the parent and final review open. Since dependencies/proof fields are create-only in the inspected API, use explicit supersession/dependency remapping when a structural contract change is unavoidable rather than putting a new contract in comments. Do not cancel history until the successor is created and verified.

After a correction, rerun all affected downstream evidence, regenerate invalidated case/assets/recordings/site derivatives and repeat independent SC-09 review. An unrelated old failure is not an excuse for new breakage. A genuine external blocker remains visible with exact evidence and next unblock; no infinite retries, false passes or silent scope reduction.
