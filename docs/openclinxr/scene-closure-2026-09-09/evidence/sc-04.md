was removed from the physician and replaced by `openclinxr_retarget_walk_formal_cc0`, bound from

geometry: 41,718 triangles before and after, every `POSITION` accessor byte-identical. The physician
GLB moves from `9ba749b2…` to `4a6d8a78…`.

**The CMU row is kept, not deleted.** It stays in the clearance table with
`shippedInRedistributedBytes: false`, so a rebuild that reintroduces a CMU clip is refused by name
rather than by absence.

**`CarnegieMellonAnimations/` is excluded by name.** Beside the CC0 clips in the same clone sits
`static/animations/CarnegieMellonAnimations/`, seven CMU `.fbx` files whose own `readme.txt` says
they are *"A small sample of FBX file from a larger library"* at `rancidmilk.itch.io`. Work the
repository did not author cannot be covered by its own CC0 dedication. The exclusion is a live
control: a clip from that directory is refused while the same record clears
`human-base-animations.glb#Walk_Formal`.

**Three sidecars now pin the bytes that ship.** Two were corrected and one was written for the first
time, and none by copying the digest off disk — `correct-sidecar-byte-lineage.ts` checks out the

## The replacement walk is measurably worse at planting its feet

Measured, not assumed. Both clips graded by the identical procedure on the same physician rig: the
foot tracks are taken in the body frame and composed with a constant ground advance derived from the
clip's own stance phases, so a clip that bakes root motion and a clip that does not are comparable.

| | CMU `02_01` (retired) | CC0 `Walk_Formal` (shipped) | CC0 `Walk` (rejected) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `toe1-1.L` slide, share of ground covered | **0.0297** | **0.2487** | 0.5401 |
| `toe1-1.L` contact frames | 54 | 18 | 20 |
| `toe1-1.R` slide share | **0.0816** | **0.2704** | 0.4724 |
| worst single frame, `toe1-1.L` | 0.0181 m | 0.0501 m | 0.0652 m |
| stance-derived ground speed | 1.265 m/s | 0.676 m/s | 0.526 m/s |
| ratio to `CLINICIAN_WALK_SPEED_MPS` | 1.150 | **0.615** | 0.478 |
| frame rate | 120 fps | 24 fps | 24 fps |

`Walk_Formal` is the better of the two CC0 candidates by a factor of two, which is why it is the one
that ships — a measurement, not a preference for its name. It is still about three times worse than
the clip it replaces, and it walks at 0.68 m/s where the executor assumes 1.1.

The licence question forced the replacement and the plant did not improve with it. Two consequences,
both recorded in `limits.unresolvedDefects`:

- SC-00 must calibrate its rubric against these numbers, not the retired clip's.
- SC-05 owns the speed decision: time-scale the clip by about 1.63, or lower the executor's constant.

Part of the gap is a narrower contact window on the CMU side — its toe is below the runtime contact
height for 54 of 345 frames (16% of the clip) against `Walk_Formal`'s 18 of 42 (43%) — so the CMU
figure is graded over less of what a viewer sees. That does not close the gap; it qualifies it.

## A defect in the bind path, found and fixed here

The first `Walk_Formal` bind reported success with 50 driven bones and a clean `BVH file(s)
retargeted` message, and put the physician's feet at 0.72–1.31 m with the legs pointing backwards.

The cause is in the addon's glTF source path. `saveGltf2Bvh` (`load.py:382`) exports the imported
action to a temporary BVH with Blender's own exporter, which writes Blender-native axes (−Y forward,
Z up), and `loadBlenderBvhFile` (`load.py:419`) re-imports it using the operator's
`@orientation_helper(axis_forward='-Z', axis_up='Y')` default. Y-up is right for a mocap `.bvh` off
disk and wrong for the addon's own intermediate, so a glTF-sourced clip arrives rotated 90° about X.

`motion_bind_stage.py` now takes an optional `--source-orientation`; passing `"Third person, Z up"`
— the addon's own name for `["-Y", "Z"]` — fixes it. With the flag absent, behaviour is unchanged.

**The same defect is in a shipped asset this card does not own.**
`mpfb-peds-parent-aisha.motion-bind.glb` carries the CC0 seated clip bound without the correction:
its `foot.L` sits at 0.49 m with the toe 0.47 m behind the knee for every frame after the rest frame.
Recorded so it is not rediscovered.

integrator. `implementation.treeClean` and `implementation.changeCommits` fail for the same reason —
the work is deliberately left uncommitted in the worktree. Re-run `build-report.ts` after the
