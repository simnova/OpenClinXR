`stepdown_room_v1` is used by `stepdown_sepsis_nurse_escalation_v1`. Seed 205 runs the registered room chain with the ward-like finish block; this is a visual/runtime factory proof, not clinical-validity, exam-equivalence, Quest-readiness, or production-readiness evidence.

The shipped GLB measured the shell opening head at **2.600 m** and the finished door+casing head at **2.155 m**, leaving a **0.445 m** opening. It emits `openclinxr_door_transom_infill` flush to the measured wall plane and uses the shell wall paint response. The ward recipe declares no transom and remains byte-identical.
