# Inpatient ward room — reference spec v2

One room. Every one of the six reference views below depicts THIS room, from a different
camera position inside it. Nothing in this spec varies between views.

## Envelope

- Interior floor plan: 8.77 m (east-west) x 7.77 m (north-south).
- Ceiling height: 2.42 m, flat suspended ceiling throughout (no soffits, no bulkheads, no
  sloped sections).
- Four walls, one door, no windows (see below). Rectangular room, square corners, flat
  walls — no bowed or curved wall surfaces anywhere.

## Finishes

- **Walls**: off-white matte painted drywall, flat and planar floor-to-ceiling. No wainscoting,
  no chair rail, no visible texture beyond ordinary paint.
- **Base**: straight grey vinyl cove base, 100 mm tall, running the full perimeter at the
  wall-floor junction. The cove base is a STRAIGHT extruded profile: flat against the wall,
  a small consistent-radius cove curve only in the last ~15 mm where it meets the floor, then
  flat onto the floor. It never bulges outward into the room, and it is never taller than
  100 mm anywhere.
- **Floor**: pale sheet-vinyl flooring, near-white warm grey, with faint linear seams every
  ~2 m (sheet width) and no other pattern. The floor is perfectly flat and level — it never
  curves upward at a wall, never forms a bench or ledge, and always meets the cove base at a
  sharp, straight junction.
- **Ceiling**: suspended acoustic ceiling. Square 600 mm x 600 mm acoustic tiles set in a
  straight white T-bar grid running parallel to the walls in both directions — grid lines are
  perfectly straight, tiles are perfectly square, and the grid does not sag, warp, or go
  out of true anywhere in the room.
- **Light fixture**: exactly one flat recessed lay-in LED troffer panel, 600 mm x 1200 mm
  (occupying a 1x2 tile module), centered in the ceiling grid roughly in the middle of the
  room. The troffer is a FLAT rectangular lens panel, flush with and coplanar to the
  surrounding ceiling tiles — it is NOT a cylindrical fixture, NOT a louvered parabolic
  fixture, and it does not hang below or sit proud of the tile plane. Its long edge is
  parallel to the door wall.

## Door

- One door only, on the **north wall** (the wall a viewer faces in view 01).
- Leaf: maple wood veneer, 0.95 m wide x 2.10 m tall, with a single narrow vertical
  vision-lite (glazed strip) positioned in the upper half of the leaf, offset toward the
  hinge side. Simple white painted door casing/trim frames the opening.
- Position: the door's centerline sits 4.885 m from the west corner of the north wall (0.50 m
  east of the wall's midpoint), leaving 3.41 m of clear wall to the door's east edge and 4.41 m
  to its west edge, after the 0.95 m leaf width (3.41 + 0.95 + 4.41 = 8.77 m, the full wall).
  Corrected 2026-09-28 from an internally inconsistent earlier draft that gave 4.20 m / west of
  midpoint for this same sentence while its own clearance figures, the "centered-right" view-01
  description, and the reference photos all agreed on an east offset; see screening.md.
- Hinges: on the **east jamb** (the right side, as seen by someone standing inside the room
  facing the door). The door swings inward, into the room, toward the west.
- The door is shown **closed** in every view. No view shows it ajar or opening into another
  space — every view is inside this one room.

## Windows

- **None.** This room has no windows on any wall. Do not add a window to any view, on any
  wall, under any lighting condition.

## Lighting

- Daylight-balanced, even, shadowless lighting throughout (the room has no windows, so this
  reads as good even ambient/fixture lighting rather than sun through glass). No colored
  gels, no dramatic shadows, no lens flare.

## Prohibited in every view

- No furniture, no people, no equipment, no clutter.
- No text, no signage, no watermarks, no logos.
- No curved walls, no curved ceiling-wall junctions, no curved floor-wall junctions beyond
  the 15 mm cove radius specified above.
- No cylindrical or louvered light fixtures.
- No non-square ceiling tiles, no bowed or misaligned T-bar grid lines.

## The six camera views

All camera heights are eye level (~1.6 m above the floor) unless stated otherwise. "Facing
north" means facing the door wall.

1. **01-toward-door** (master / canonical view). Camera stands near the room's center,
   roughly 4.4 m south of the north (door) wall and 4.4 m east of the west wall, at eye
   level, facing north directly at the door. The door appears centered-right in the frame
   per its true offset position (4.885 m from the west corner), fully visible, closed, hinge
   on its right (east) side as seen from this view. Ceiling grid, troffer and both side walls
   are visible converging toward the door wall in correct one-point perspective toward a
   single vanishing point near the door.

2. **02-toward-bed-wall**. Camera stands at the same spot as view 01 but rotated 180°, facing
   south at the bare wall opposite the door (the wall where a hospital bed would sit). The
   door is behind the camera, out of frame. Same floor, base, ceiling grid and troffer
   finishes, same vanishing point geometry mirrored to this wall.

3. **03-ceiling-corner**. Camera stands in the room's southwest floor corner, tilted up
   steeply, looking diagonally toward the room's center and the troffer. Both the south and
   west walls and their straight, square top corner are visible in the lower frame; the flat
   troffer sits mid-ceiling, flush in the square-tile T-bar grid, with straight grid lines
   receding correctly to the vanishing point. No soffit, bulkhead, or ceiling height change
   anywhere in frame.

4. **04-door-inside**. Camera stands 1.5 m south of the door, on-axis with it (same hinge
   side, same position, same wall as view 01 and view 03), facing north, closer than view 01
   so the maple leaf, white casing, and vertical vision-lite read clearly at larger scale.
   The door is closed. This is the same physical door as every other view: same wall, same
   4.885 m offset, same east-jamb hinge, same inward swing.

5. **05-troffer-junction**. Camera stands under the troffer, tilted up, framed tight on where
   one long edge of the flat troffer lens meets the surrounding acoustic tile and T-bar grid.
   Framed away from any wall or corner so no wall-ceiling junction is visible — ceiling only,
   flat and flush, straight grid lines, square tiles.
- Cropped close enough that the flat troffer face and the coplanar tile grid dominate the
   frame.

6. **06-floor-base**. Camera is low, close to the floor, framed on a straight stretch of wall
   away from any corner, showing the pale sheet-vinyl floor meeting the straight 100 mm grey
   cove base at a sharp, flat junction. The floor is flat to the frame's edge — no upward
   curve, no bench shape, no ledge.
