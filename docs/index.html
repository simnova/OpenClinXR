OpenClinXR

Inspired by Step 2 CS-style multi-station flow: a practice circuit where clinical educators author cases, learners run timed encounters, and faculty review every turn on the record.

OpenClinXR gives clinical educators an open-source (MIT, code), local-first factory that compiles inspectable case blueprints into seeded multi-station WebXR practice encounters with replayable traces and auditable debrief records.

One case blueprint. A multi-station practice circuit. Every turn on the record.

What we own

  • Open source (MIT, code), no licence fee.

  • One case blueprint casts each station: actors, dialogue seeds, rubric and trace come from the scenario.

  • A synced multi-station circuit with breaks, a patient note, a read-only observer, replay, rubric marks entered over the protocol, and a sign-off packet that locks them (protocol today; faculty review UI pending).

  • Local-first: the synced server and client have no language-model dependency, actor speech is synthesized in the browser, and traces, notes and marks are files on the host.

  • Each turn is recorded with modality and confidence; replay captions are recomputed from the trace.

What is still behind

  • No validation study.

  • 15 fixtures, 14 at draft status, authored topic answers for ED chest pain only.

  • No LTI (Learning Tools Interoperability), single sign-on, or xAPI (Experience API) integration.

  • No accreditation or standards crosswalk.

  • Avatar realism below commercial art.

  • No worn-headset run, no support organisation.

  • No physical examination or touch is assessed.

  • Rubric items are labels without definitions or anchors.
