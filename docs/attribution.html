Shipping under a recorded assumption — disclosed, not resolved

Three components ship where the source page and the file header disagree about the licence. Each ships under a dated, named ruling recorded in the ledger rather than a silent decision, and each is listed here so the assumption is visible to anyone relying on this work.

Because of these, public render rights for the scene-closure cast are blocked and no imagery from that work is published. If an upstream owner confirms that a copyleft header is the real grant, every asset derived from it will be re-baked without it.

Recorded refusals

The ledger records assets that were acquired, measured, and then refused — among them two MakeHuman skin packs whose pack pages claim CC0 while individual files carry explicit AGPLv3 headers or no licence line, and Mixamo, whose terms forbid redistributing raw animation files. Unspecified is treated as a refusal. Those refusals are kept in the ledger by name so a later rebuild cannot quietly reintroduce them.

Corrections welcome. If you authored something listed here and the attribution or the licence reading is wrong, please open an issue on the repository and it will be corrected and re-baked.
