## Source

- Repo-authored local Blender fixtures generated by
  `tools/openclinxr/evidence/medical-equipment-artifacts.ts` (emits Blender Python)
  — applies to `ecg-cart-12-lead.glb` and `iv-pole-with-pump.glb`.
- Runtime path: apps/ui-xr/public/xr-assets/medical-equipment/.
- Generator default output root (when not overridden):
  .openclinxr/asset-production/ed-chest-pain/medical-equipment/.
- Issue 168: ECG cart part placement was fixed so casters, cabinet, screen, shelf, and
  lead bundle form one assembled object after glTF export (world-matrix contact).
- `wall-clock-analog.glb` is a **TRELLIS derivative** (issue #239): generated by the
  TRELLIS.2 Metal image-to-shape-to-mesh-to-GLB pipeline
  (`pedronaugusto/trellis2-apple`, MIT) on Apple Silicon, decimated to the r0.02 rung
  (34,507 tris), graded and published as evidence (issue #243), then promoted
  byte-identical here (issue #244). Full chain in the sidecar
  `wall-clock-analog.provenance.json`.
- `bedside-monitor-generated.glb` is a **TRELLIS derivative** (issues #237/#239/#250):
  generated by the same TRELLIS.2 Metal pipeline, decimated to 60,000 tris via the
  issue-250 global-simplify control (silhouette IoU 0.9854, exterior area 0.9992),
  then promoted byte-identical here (issue #253). Full chain in the sidecar
  `bedside-monitor-generated.provenance.json`.

## Policy

- Local fixture only.
- No external assets, cloud APIs, or paid APIs. `wall-clock-analog.glb` and
  `bedside-monitor-generated.glb` are the exceptions to "third-party generated assets":
  they are generated locally by the MIT-licensed TRELLIS.2 pipeline (no network
  generation), with licence chain and derivative lineage recorded in their provenance
  sidecars.
- No production asset readiness, Quest readiness, clinical validity, or scoring claim.
- Primitive in-scene parametric equipment geometry remains the fallback for kinds
  without a real GLB.
