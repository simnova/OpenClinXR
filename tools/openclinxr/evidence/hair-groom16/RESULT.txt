REMAINING (report-only per brief; no offsets added to make gates pass):
  - The FALSIFIER is the finding: the Blender Essentials Interpolate children do not
    respect the body; the groom15 Shrinkwrap was the collision control and it flattens
    depth. A station that wants both needs a real shape-preserving collision (project
    each child's shape, not each sample) or a different child generator. Cleared guides
    (this slice) remove the guide-side penetration but do not fix the child side.
  - Guide clearance itself PASSES and is native to the guide field; it is the correct
    upstream hygiene and should be kept regardless of the child stage.

    p90 14.93 still overshoots. std gates pass, so the spread is there but shifted.
  - pinna 159719 and concha/faceMask need an ear/tuck + face-region pass independent of
    the child generator.
