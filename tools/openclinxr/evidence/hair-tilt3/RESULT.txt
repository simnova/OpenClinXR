SAW::The inferred reading half-confirmed again, from the other side: a
pure long wave does NOT break the bands (L240 374, L450 391, L450x6 460
back bands — the slope still oscillates about zero along the strand, so
adjacent highlight rows shift in opposite directions and re-merge, same
as tilt2). The lean-return DOES break them (646 x 22.4, closest to TS
short of tilt1), because each fibre holds its own lean over ~60% of its
run — but the displacement cost returns with it (p95 26.5 mm mid-strand,
eroded +36%, eye -3.5 pp), even with tip displacement exactly zero.
Against TS the first wrong read is unchanged: the back curtain keeps
coarse bands (best 646 x 22 px vs TS 849 x 16 px) and the front curtain
has no slack left (every lean variant widens the eroded mask and moves
the honest part/side). The mechanism is now isolated: fragmentation
needs sustained per-fibre lean over tens of cm; silhouette cost scales
with the displacement that lean accumulates. Next attempt should make
the lean bundle-coherent (neighbours share theta, so the silhouette
moves as a sheet instead of fraying) or schedule amplitude by view
(back only), not per-fibre everywhere.
