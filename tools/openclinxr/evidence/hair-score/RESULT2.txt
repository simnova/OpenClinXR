HAIR SCORE v2 — ORIENTATION AND BAND METRICS

WHY::Prior gates measured shape (silhouette IoU) and brightness/colour only; tangent changes were invisible. Research practice (Nam et al. CVPR 2019 strand capture; Paris et al.) compares Gabor orientation maps inside the hair mask; the sheet look is photometric (lighting-band count).

PARAMS::Gabor bank 180 orientations at 1 deg steps; kernel 15x15 px, wavelength 4.0 px, sigma 3.0, gamma 0.5, psi 0. Wavelength 4 px = the 2-4 px strand/ridge period at native 1024 px (compared at the native judge frame; downsampling would halve the tuned period). Sigma 3.0 fills the 15 px kernel to ~2.5 sigma (negligible truncation, cheap filter2D). Gamma 0.5 elongates the envelope along the strand = direction selectivity (1.0 would be a blob detector). Psi 0 (even) = ridge detector, maximal on strand centres. Confidence = (peak-mean)/(peak+eps) over orientations. Scores inside the intersection of the two hair masks: confidence-product-weighted mean |angular diff| (mod 180) + 18-bin histogram EMD (binWidth*sum|CDF diff|, degrees). Registration = judge v6 register_view (similarity, no rotation), render triples from hair-curtain3/landmarks.json, target triples from judge target-set-back/landmarks.json with the judge's skull-mode +10 mm front offset (H_mm=210.659); three_quarter identity (judge has no 3q registration). Filtering restricted to the intersection bbox (+8 px margin ring dropped; kernel radius 7) — exact on kept pixels, ~10x faster. Bands: cv2 8-way components above hair-mask p90 in back box (320,280,704,664) on back view and ear box (100,240,484,624) on three_quarter; column-luminance autocorrelation peak/lag (lags 2..191) after 41 px boxcar detrending (raw profile is trend-dominated: lag-2 peak 0.85-0.96 everywhere). score.json schema openclinxr.hair-score.v2 (v1 keys untouched); keys `orientation` (per view) + `bands` (back/ear, ours vs target).
