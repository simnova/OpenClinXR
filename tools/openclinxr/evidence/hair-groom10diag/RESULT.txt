CONCLUSION
- The candidate explanation "SDF projection / keep-out caused all defects"
  is falsified.  Those stages often make large later moves, but every defect
  is plainly present in raw interpolation.  Knot and profile loops originate
  in guide paths; tuft and hairline band originate when sparse guides are
  expanded into dense children.  Projection/clump amplify rather than first
  produce the four named defects.
