dive-through is not "Follow Surface Normal"; it is the number of guides the
Interpolate blend averages. Nearest-guide-only is the least-bad setting and

3. CHOICE AND WHY (card item 2)
-------------------------------
No cell qualifies (inside < 1% AND parietal+temple p50/p90 within +-20%):
0 / 24. Best inside: fsn1_cls3_ig1_dtg0.02 -> 5.90% inside, 7.34% need>3mm,
parietal p50 ratio 0.601, temple p50 ratio 0.578 (both far below the +-20%
band). Best need>3mm: fsn1_cls1_ig1_dtg0.02 -> 5.23%, still >2%.
=> stop with the table (valid result per card).

Variable that moves inside% most: INTERPOLATION GUIDES (13.11-pt marginal
range; 2.7x from ig1 to ig3). Mechanism: Interpolate averages the shape of the
N nearest guides; with more guides a child's samples are pulled toward guide
points rooted elsewhere on the curved skull, bending the strand through the
body. Nearest-guide-only (ig1) is the only setting that materially reduces
penetration, and it also raises temple p50 (3.15 mm vs 2.22 mm at ig3,
cls3/dtg0.02) - i.e. it helps depth AND collision together by weakening the
cross-guide blend. But ig1 still leaves 5.9% inside and p50 at ~0.58-0.60 of
the guide reference, so even the best setting is not a fix.

Two hypotheses from the card, adjudicated:
  H1 (leading) "Follow Surface Normal = 0 makes root-relative world-offset
     children point into a curved skull" -> REFUTED as the cause. fsn 0->1
     moves inside% 0.60 pts (15.58 -> 14.98) and need>3mm 1.98 pts; it does
     not cross under 1% in any row. It also WORSENS bangs 1.5x (100.0 -> 149.2)
     and temple p90 at cls3 (15.29 -> 17.48). Not the lever.
  H2 "3 depth classes leave fewer, farther guides per class (groom12 one class
     had no bangs)" -> REFUTED as a penetration cause: cls3 lowers inside%
     slightly (15.92 -> 14.65) and does not raise need>3mm (24.50 vs 24.49).
     It IS the bangs driver (mean 5.5 -> 243.7 samples; cls3 with ig1 pushes
     a forward fringe across the forehead). So depth classes affect SHAPE
     (bangs), not penetration.

Causal summary: penetration scales with how many guides the Interpolate blend
averages. That is a property of the node, not of the guides (which already
pass the 0-sample < 1.5 mm gate) and not of Follow Surface Normal. It confirms
groom16 and adds a monotone dose-response (ig1<ig3<ig6).

6. SCOPE / NOT TESTED / REMAINING
---------------------------------
SCOPE: local Blender build lane only, low density (~61k children, 1/4.6 of the
groom16 product). No render, no scorer, no Quest/WebXR runtime, no on-headset
evidence, no downstream (arena/iwsdk) load. The matrix varies the Interpolate
stage only; Clump/Noise/Smooth/Shrinkwrap/outside-pass were held OFF so the
stage under test is isolated (D4).
NOT TESTED: full-density behaviour of the winning setting (density may shift
the exact percentages); whether Clump/Noise/Smooth re-introduce or mask
penetration; the groom16 hard gates; worn-headset / WebXR clip-through; whether
the outside-pass spikes are stable under animation; interaction with the
Shrinkwrap control groom15 used; other Interpolate knobs not in the grid
(distribution method, density mask, part-by-islands off).
REMAINING:
  - The Interpolate stage has no setting in this grid that keeps children out
    of the skull (<1%). Nearest-guide (ig1) + distance 0.02 + cls3 is the
    least-bad cell (5.90% inside, 7.34% need>3mm) and should be the input to
    any future fix, but it is not itself a fix.
  - The fix must change the geometry model, not the Interpolate parameters:
    either a shape-preserving collision (project each child's SHAPE, not each
    sample - groom16 section 6) or a child generator that does not average
    across distant guides (e.g. per-guide strand copies in the guide's own
    local frame, then a bounded surface projection), or a guide-density
    increase high enough that ig1 is well-sampled everywhere.
  - Guide clearance (groom16) remains correct and is kept as the input.
  - Bangs are a depth-class/interp-guides effect; any forward-fringe fix
    (hairline guides, forehead tuck) is independent of the penetration fix.
