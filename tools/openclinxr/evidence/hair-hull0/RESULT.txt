METHOD (S6/hull.py; reuse by import, no copies)
- Station projector: envelope.project_to_render with the pinned
  cameras-scorer-v3.json (matrixWorld + lens + sensor verbatim).
- Registration: register.similarity_from_triples via instruments
  (render -> target frame). Registration p95 = 0.0 px: the station
  similarity is an exact 3-point fit, residuals da=db=dc=0.0 on all three
  views (/tmp/station0b-run/registration.json). Masks dilated by
  (0.0 + 2) = 2 px (binary_dilation, cross structuring, 2 iterations).
  Projector verification worst 0.469 px and landmark confidence 2-5 px are
  printed, never folded into the dilation.
- Occlusion: occl0 method — hair-cage1/body-depth.npz render-frame
  cover+depth (station0b bare body + garment, library topper excluded);
  per-voxel visibility against the +/-2 px patch median with the occl0
  5 mm margin (visible iff voxel depth < patch median - 5 mm, else
  BODY_OCCLUDED). Occlusion takes precedence over the mask test.
- Body removal: hair-cage1/body-exact-sdf.npz (Blender BVH exact field);
  voxels with SDF <= 2.0 mm removed.
- Scalp contact: scalp1 scalp mesh (scalp.npz verts+tris, 778 tris);
  voxels within 4.0 mm of a scalp triangle (per-triangle bbox +
  point-triangle distance) count as touching. scalp1 roots used for the
  proximity diagnostic only.
- Carve rule, incl. closing-added voxels: reject on any VISIBLE_OUTSIDE.
