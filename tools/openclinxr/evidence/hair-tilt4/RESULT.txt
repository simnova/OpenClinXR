SAW::Against TS the first wrong read is unchanged in kind but narrower in
extent: the back curtain keeps coarse bands (best 560 x 25.7 px vs TS
849 x 16.4 px; the gap is now a pure fragmentation deficit, no longer
bundled with a silhouette/IoU deficit). The three ideas resolved cleanly:
(a) rear-only scheduling WORKS for the silhouette — eroded mask
+0.4/+0.5/+1.8% vs LR's +36%, eye/jaw bit-identical, honest part/side
unmoved, all four cells pass IoU+ori; but rear-only INDEPENDENT lean
reaches only 560 bands vs LR's 646, so the curtain was contributing ~90
bands of LR's fragmentation. (b) bundle-coherence KILLS the effect —
378 bands, barely above base 353: neighbours leaning together move the
sheet as a whole and the highlight rows re-merge, same mechanism as the
pure wave. Coherence is the wrong direction for fragmentation; the
fragmentation REQUIRES independent per-fibre lean. (c) outline
compensation is honest but nearly a no-op — rear p99 half-widths move
<=2.5 mm under rear-only lean, so ratios sit at 0.98-1.0 and comp gains
only +49 bands over coh while restoring nothing that was lost (nothing
was lost). The isolated mechanism stands: fragmentation = sustained
INDEPENDENT per-fibre lean over tens of cm; silhouette cost scales with
WHERE the lean is applied (curtain vs rear), not with coherence. Next
attempt should keep independent rear-only lean and raise its strength
where the silhouette is blind to it (mid-sheet amplitude schedule, or
sigma between 4 and 6 rear-only independent), not coherent or compensated.
