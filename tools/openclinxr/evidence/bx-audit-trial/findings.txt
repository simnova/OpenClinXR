Verification method
- z-fighting: re-measured each flagged pair's plane separation and the texel each face shows. Runtime depth: near 0.1, far 100 (apps/ui-xr/src/main.ts:2888), 24-bit, so a 10 um gap resolves out to ~4 m. Only coincident pairs (<1 um) with UV delta >0.01 can show. For those, A/B Workbench render deleting one side vs the other, same camera.
- inward faces: every flagged material is doubleSided=true; three.js flips back-face normals, so nothing is culled.
- floating: highlighted renders.

Precision: 2 confirmed defects in 30 GLBs (jeans pocket, IV pump). ~25 error lines out of ~85 are z-fighting, nearly all FP at runtime depth precision because eps = 0.01% of the bounding diagonal (0.2 mm on a human).

Visemes: bx_rig.VISEMES_15 = (sil PP FF TH DD kk CH SS nn RR aa E I O U) equals mouth-verifier PROBE_OVR_TOKENS (packages/openclinxr/stations/mouth-verifier/src/probe.ts:36) as a set. Our morph names carry a `viseme_` prefix, so shape_report needs a rename map.

Decision: decline vendoring as a station gate. Floating-part check is the one with signal; a station gate would need eps tied to runtime depth precision, a doubleSided-aware winding check, and per-prop gating, which is most of the code.
