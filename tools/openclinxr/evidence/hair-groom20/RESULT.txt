  bind positional divergence once the 20 mm graph mixes opposing fall
  vectors; anchor 0.3 lets the neighbourhood win and swaps fall vectors
  (the groom19-rejected silhouette collapse, here BACK-only but leaking
  into the front via below-vertex side-neutral blending). Groom11-style
  positional (offset-diffusion) work, or a part-corridor re-trace that
  separates the two diagonal sheets at the guide level, is the tool for
  positional coherence -- out of scope (tracer frozen). Alternatively
  redefine the gate to post-take-off divergence, as groom19 noted.
- Silhouette collapse (front -0.28 / 3q -0.33 / profile -0.16 /
  back -0.13): the BACK explosion over-covers three views and starves
  the right hem. Needs the fairing reverted (anchor back to ~0.7,
  single pass, or BACK graph split by fall direction as well as part
  side) -- i.e. this card's exact prescription is measured not-viable.
- R-LOOP 87k/101k vs 11587 (same-stack 8a 55k/64k): winding lives in
  guide paths (turn-gated <= 5 deg/step but 400+ deg total); needs
  guide-side unwind of the named region guides, or fewer interior blends.
- Face 3q/face-front over-cover (front 11602 vs 3424.9; bangs 42,034):
