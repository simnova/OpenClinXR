4. ANSWER (one paragraph)
-------------------------
The 2D projection metric counts layering, not locks: only 10-22% of its
pairs are within 3 mm in 3D (8a 22.2%, 19 18.8%, 21-family ~10%), and the
3D lens shows position fairing worked 2.5x better than the 2D gate could
see (true crossings 2258->753, -67%, vs 2D -27%); but it cannot score
groom12, which has no guides file. The card-specified image metric
(Gabor vs-target orientation deviation in the back-knot box) does NOT
separate the known-good groom12 from defective 8a/19 -- 12 reads mid-pack
on every orientation aggregate tested (~14 variants: gates, weighting,
flow-scale, dispersion, cross-scale, coherent components, histograms),
because fine Blender-children texture and lock bands produce the same
pixel-level disagreement and all grooms share the photo's flow mismatch;
the only image aggregate that puts 12 with the target and all three
defectives apart is the sigma-2 gradient-anisotropy ratio gy/gx
(target 0.53, 12 0.55, 8b 0.85, 19 1.02, 8a 1.27), whose lock-vs-fineness
mechanism is still unproven. The locks' guide sources in groom19 are the
adjacent upper-back guides 393/378/357 (+2278/2279).
