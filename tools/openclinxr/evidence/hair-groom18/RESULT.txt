HANDBACK ITEMS (done this turn):
- Checked E/ + strands/height_transfer.py: prior workers left strands-18-pre.npz
  (Attach/Interp/Clump/Noise children) and strands-18.npz plus an UNBOUNDED
  transfer run (build/height-transfer.json: insideBody 3; 78% of children moved
  > 3 mm; child max-move p90 35 mm, max 773 mm path).
- Investigated the 773 mm outlier: it is a far-field gradient fault. The
  unbounded walker sized each step as 0.8 * (target - SDF) along the unit
  gradient, which assumes the target isosurface lies along the gradient at
  distance ~= deficit. Deep samples (pre-transfer SDF down to -60 mm, e.g.
  child 125602 pre -60.78 mm -> target +36.08 mm) overshoot across thin
  structures, the gradient flips, and the walk ping-pongs: worst sample path
  773.27 mm for a net displacement of only 123.68 mm (recomputed). resid max
  was 38.24 mm (non-converged oscillators).
- Bounded the step in height_transfer.py: per-iteration step cap 10 mm,
  per-sample cumulative path cap 150 mm (capped samples stop and are counted),
  best-residual position tracked per sample and restored at the end so
  oscillation never leaves a sample worse than its closest approach, active-set
  sampling only. Re-ran via E/rerun_transfer18.py (same function the Blender
  --height-transfer path calls; pure numpy, no RNG).
- Bounded result: path max exactly 150.0 (cap binds), net max 97.81;
  resid p50/p90/p99 0.019/0.041/0.049, max 28.27 (capped samples that could
  not reach target); postInside 0, postBelowMin 0 (was 3/6). 3229 samples /
  543 children (0.22%) hit the total cap; 1,507,787 samples hit the per-iter
  cap at least once (expected: deep samples need many 10 mm steps).

6. SCOPE / NOT TESTED / REMAINING
-----------------------------------
SCOPE: local Blender render lane only. No Quest/WebXR runtime, no on-headset
evidence, no performance/frame-budget measurement, no downstream consumer
(arena/iwsdk) load.
NOT TESTED: worn-headset validation; WebXR capture; clip-through in-engine;
whether the transferred shell is stable under animation; Clump<=8mm tail
(disp max 58.3 mm exceeds the step-1 spec — untested whether a lower clump
factor keeps depth while fixing the tail).
REMAINING (report-only per brief; no offsets added to make gates pass):
- The mechanism works at the sample level (resid p99 0.05 mm, inside 0) but
  large radial moves along a spatially varying gradient relocate strands:
  pre-transfer bangs 0 -> post 336 proves exit-point drift. "Tangential
  position untouched" holds per-step, not per-strand: a 97 mm walk that
  changes direction exits the body somewhere the strand never belonged
  (forehead, ears). The next card is either guide-side (render the cleared
  guides themselves: if THEY already spill onto face/ears, heights are wrong
  for the target) or transfer-side (constrain the exit: face/ear-region
  masks, or project each child's SHAPE rather than each sample).
- The falsifier as written did not fire (temple p90 misses by 0.08 mm), so
  no guide-side verdict is declared; but front below groom16's floor with
  depth essentially met keeps guide-side as the leading hypothesis.
- pinna (10272), concha, faceMask, backCore (+0.0064), bangs need a
  placement/region pass independent of the height mechanism.
- Clump tail (max 58.3 mm vs 8 mm spec) should be re-measured with lower
  clump factors; Noise was not isolated from Clump in disp18.json.
