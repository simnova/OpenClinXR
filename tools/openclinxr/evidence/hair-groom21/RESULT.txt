guides (35,245 pairs). Recorded in every fairing.json ("input").

Gate: <= 30% of 9715 = <= 2914. FAIL (over by 4503 pairs).

20 mm-kernel local mean cannot bind crossings between guides whose
neighbourhoods never interact; it cut 2298 pairs (-23.6%) -- real
structure response, not noise -- but 76.4% remain.
