## What ran

A `selected_to_active` normal bake, 296,226-tri raw source to the 79,999-tri champion, 2048², Cycles. I reused the existing bake tooling at `automate_blender.py:1362`, which is configured for a procedural-to-texture bake (`use_selected_to_active = False`); high-poly to low-poly transfer is the same API with `selected_to_active` plus a cage. Cage extrusion was derived from geometry: 2% of the object's measured 1.334 m diagonal.

The map carries detail. Of the 2048² texels, 42.9% deviate from flat, mean deviation 16.08, max 182, blue channel 247 (correct for tangent space). That measurement was the point of the check, since a bake that reports success while writing a flat 128/128/255 map is the silent failure here.
