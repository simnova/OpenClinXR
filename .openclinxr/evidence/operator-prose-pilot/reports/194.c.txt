Baking has not been tried on MPFB, and the TRELLIS result does not transfer.

The OpenAI consult stalled at 241 KB with no further growth, so nothing here incorporates it. Every number below is geometry or bytes. Nothing about how any of this looks was tested. The Quest triangle budget motivating the work is stated in the rules file and has never been validated on hardware.

The constraint that decides the design: all 11 MPFB assets already ship a normal map, and that map is not flat (mean luminance 142.1 against a flat-map value of 142.5, std dev 5.75–8.01). The existing procedural-bump bake carries skin micro-detail. A high→low form bake wants the same slot, so the real question is whether to replace skin detail with form or combine both into one map.

Setup difference: TRELLIS decimates (`iterate-optimize.ts`, meshopt) and its bake recovered form that decimation destroyed. MPFB never decimates; grep for decimate|simplify|meshopt returns nothing, `use_selected_to_active = False` at **automate_blender.py:1350** with no cage, and TANGENT is absent in 11 of 11 assets. So the goal here is to bake in order to enable a decimation we do not currently perform.

Scale: 11 MPFB humanoids total 980,913 triangles and 135.1 MB; a station staging 3–4 actors carries 225–300k humanoid triangles.
