## What ran

A `selected_to_active` normal bake, 296,226-tri raw source onto the 79,999-tri champion, 2048², Cycles. Reused the existing bake tooling at `automate_blender.py:1362` rather than hand-rolling; that path is a procedural-to-texture bake with `use_selected_to_active = False`, so the transfer needed selected-to-active plus a cage. Cage extrusion derived from geometry at 2% of the object's measured 1.334 m diagonal, not a chosen number.

Map is not flat: 42.9% of texels deviate from neutral, mean deviation 16.08, max 182, blue channel 247 (correct for tangent space). A bake that reports success and writes flat 128/128/255 is the silent failure mode here, so that check was the point.
