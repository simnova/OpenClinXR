Expression and emotion are not driven at runtime. `scenario-runtime.ts` instantiates a per-actor `EmotionEngine` from `resolveCaseEmotionPolicy`, so case definitions already carry emotional state and transitions, but `EmotionEngine` appears nowhere in `apps/ui-xr`. The 32 face unit names appear only in provenance files, a resolver, a census tool and tests. Both ends exist and nothing joins them. This is the incomplete-loop shape this repo has hit six times: a mechanism built and never called.

NOT TESTED: whether the 32 face units deform visibly when driven. Nothing has ever set them, so their amplitude is unmeasured; the census tool measures presence, not motion.
