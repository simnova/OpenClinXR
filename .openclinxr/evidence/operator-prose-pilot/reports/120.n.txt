**MakeClothes was already integrated and shipping before this session.** The main finding is that most of my effort went into the wrong consumer.

## The correction that mattered

Two consumers exist. `fit_stage.py` (481 lines) is reached only by an opt-in comparator flag; `materialize_mpfb_humanoid_candidate.py` (5,344 lines) is what the live cast uses. I improved the former. Lines 25–28 of the production materializer already recorded the `culturalibre_male_boots` CC-0 licence and the `< 13,380` binding constraint I rediscovered slowly — while #540's refusal of those boots stood unchallenged. They ship on two bodies at 30,768 triangles.

## Landed

- Two licence refusals reversed. The `.obj` `# license AGPL3` header is stale boilerplate pointing at a parked domain; `.mhclo` and pack page say CC-0. Precedence now documented: asset descriptor → publisher pack page → mesh header, asset-specific only. `skins01/02` remain refused (explicit per-file AGPLv3).
- Fit station builds its body with MPFB (`c2273bca`); fixed unstripped helper "robe" geometry and an importer Y-up→Z-up object rotation. Four wrong diagnoses A/B'd out first.
- 400-line skill, opening with the two-consumer trap.

## Open

`--body-glb` is impossible as scoped; abandoned pre-implementation. GLB export splits seams (13,380 → 16,048), destroying index ordering — and many indices stay in range, so ClothesService would silently fit wrong vertices. Helper-stripping is safe (max index 11,018).

**Presence ≠ fit quality.** #659 measured 37.8% of one foot outside its shoe; `toigo_flats` ships 57,600 triangles from a 28,800-face source. Fit quality is ungraded.

NOT TESTED: whether the production materializer shares the helper-shell or rotation defects. Its output renders correctly, but I did not read its code.
