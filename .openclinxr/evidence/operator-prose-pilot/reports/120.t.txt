`--body-glb` (clothing an already-materialized body) is impossible as scoped and was abandoned before implementation. A `.mhclo` binds to basemesh vertex indices; GLB export splits seams (13,380 to 16,048 vertices) and destroys the ordering. Many indices stay numerically in range, so ClothesService would fit wrong vertices rather than fail a bounds check. Helper-stripping is safe (this garment's highest index is 11,018), so export is the destructive boundary.
