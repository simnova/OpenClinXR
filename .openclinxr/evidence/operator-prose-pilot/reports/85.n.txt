## Adopt three

**1. The bake-before-decimate *ordering*.** We already do both — room bakes (`openclinxr_room_bake_*`) and meshopt decimation (`trellis-monitor-decimation`) — but as separate pipelines on different assets, never as an ordered sequence on one. Capturing high-poly detail into a texture before discarding geometry is what makes aggressive decimation survivable.

**2. Mesh merging.** The clearest gap: `external-tool-cagematch.ts:79` declares `drawCalls?: number` as a measured field and there is **no merging implementation anywhere** — `bpy.ops.object.join` and `mergeGeometries` both return zero hits. We measure a cost we never reduce.

**3. Texture atlasing.** Exists only as prose ("can be re-lit, re-tinted, or atlased afterwards"), never generated. This and merging are one change, not two — merged meshes with separate textures still cost draw calls.
