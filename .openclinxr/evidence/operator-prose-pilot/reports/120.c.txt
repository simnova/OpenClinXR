I improved fit_stage.py. Lines 25-28 of the production materializer already recorded the `culturalibre_male_boots` CC-0 licence and the <13,380 binding constraint, both rediscovered the slow way, while #540's refusal of those boots stood unchallenged. Those boots ship on two bodies at 30,768 triangles.

`--body-glb` (clothing an already-materialized body) is impossible as scoped and was abandoned before implementation. A .mhclo binds to basemesh vertex indices, and GLB export splits at seams (13,380 to 16,048), destroying the ordering. Many indices stay numerically in range, so ClothesService would fit the wrong vertices rather than fail a bounds check. Helper-stripping is safe: this garment's highest index is 11,018.

Fit quality is ungraded. #659 measured 37.8% of one foot outside its shoe while the same code fitted another actor correctly; toigo_flats ships 57,600 triangles from a 28,800-face authored source.
